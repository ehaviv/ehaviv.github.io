Sudoku Helper - FAQs

How do I change the difficulty level? +
On the main screen, tap "New Game". Select Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert level to generate a new puzzle grid.

Cryptarithm Solver - FAQs

How do I synchronize my progress? +
Go to the Profile section inside the app menu and toggle the "Google Play Games Cloud Save" option to link your live data progress automatically.

Regex Pattern Tester - FAQs

Does this app support flavor-specific regex? +
Yes, you can toggle between Standard JavaScript, Python, and PCRE flavors inside the editor settings menu.

Bulls and Cows - FAQs

What is the difference between a Bull and a Cow? +
A "Bull" means a digit is correct and in the right position. A "Cow" means a digit is correct but in the wrong position.

Hit Miss Game - FAQs

How do I reset my high score statistics? +
Navigate to Settings from the main dashboard, scroll down, and tap on "Clear Local History Data".
